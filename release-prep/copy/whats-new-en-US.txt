The TabForge app now sets up your keyboard. Settings, themes and snippets you change in the app's Playground, Layouts and Themes reach the keyboard the next time it opens in another app. Changes you make from the keyboard's gear menu stay in the keyboard, and for each setting the newer change wins. Everything stays on your device, and TabForge still doesn't ask for Full Access.

Haptic feedback now shows that it needs Full Access, which iOS requires before a keyboard can vibrate. Key click sounds work as before.
