TabForge 1.0.1 (13): optional Tip Jar

Open the companion app, then Settings > Tip Jar. Five optional one-time tips support development. They unlock no features and do not start a subscription. All app and keyboard features remain free to use.

TestFlight purchases use Apple's test environment and do not charge you. Before confirming, check that the system purchase sheet identifies the test environment. No production payment is needed for testing.

Please try:
- Load all five localized prices, leave the screen and return, then try the same tip more than once.
- Close Apple's confirmation without purchasing. The app should stay usable and allow another attempt.
- Check the menu with large text, light/dark appearance, phone portrait/landscape, iPad and supported Duo layouts.
- If purchases are restricted or a product cannot load, the menu should explain the problem without affecting the keyboard.
- Continue ordinary typing, snippets and optional swipe input. The Tip Jar should not change keyboard behavior or permissions.

Apple handles payment and transaction records. TabForge has no purchase backend and does not save a receipt, purchase history or tip total. The keyboard still does not request Full Access or share its local settings with the companion app.

Use synthetic examples in feedback. Local StoreKit and simulator checks do not establish sandbox availability, physical-device behavior or App Review approval.
