The iOS Standard theme now matches Apple's keyboard. The system's glass background shows through translucent keys with the system font, Go, Search and Send get a blue return key, and the keyboard follows light and dark mode as soon as they change. The Darcula, Dracula and Nord themes are unchanged.

Switching keyboards is easier. TabForge keeps its globe key in view on iPhone Duo, on landscape iPhones and on iPad, and checks again after you rotate or fold. Tap the globe for the next keyboard, or hold it to choose from the list. Next keyboard is also the first item in the gear menu, and Layout & keys > Toolbar keys can add a globe beside the gear.

On landscape iPhones and a closed Duo turned on its side, tips and messages now appear over the space bar instead of covering the top row of letters.
